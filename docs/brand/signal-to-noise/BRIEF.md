# Signal to Noise masthead

Eddie requested a distinctive wordmark with a symbol after rejecting the plain
text heading. This is the personal ID8Labs newsletter; Shipped is the magazine.

Essence: finding useful patterns and clear ideas amid excess information.
Audience: builders and readers of Eddie's essays. Editorial, intelligent, warm,
and recognizable; avoid generic SaaS, podcast, and AI sparkle conventions.

Use the existing editorial palette: warm paper #fafaf7, ink #0b0b0b, orange #ff6b35.
Prefer a serif wordmark that fits Fraunces with a compact standalone symbol.
The primary role is the newsletter archive masthead; secondary roles are issue
pages and email header. Must remain legible in monochrome and at small sizes.

Compare four directions before selecting and vectorizing a production mark:
clear carrier among noise, sifting aperture, editorial S/N monogram, and a
restrained signal stamp. Review the rendered concepts with Iris. The current
live heading remains in place until a reviewed replacement is ready.
