# Concept review

Iris recommends 01, Clear frequency. Several irregular paths resolve into one
precise line, making the publication's meaning visible. The stacked serif name
feels warm and editorial. Provisional concept score: 23/28.

Refine the symbol to approximately 75% of its current relative scale so it
supports the name. Tighten optical spacing in the wordmark. Keep three irregular
paths and a straight exit; avoid turning it into audio branding.

03, S/N is the runner-up: editorial presence but weaker conceptual meaning.
02, The filter reads as a lens/scientific diagram; its dots become fragile small.
04, Selected signal resembles an app grid and feels more like a magazine masthead.

For 01, monochrome should turn the exit black without losing the metaphor. A
dedicated micro mark should maintain approximately 1.5px strokes and 2px gaps at
16px, then be inspected in actual light/dark raster proofs. The generated board
does not establish font fidelity, exact palette, vector quality or 16px legibility.

## Selected direction: 04

Eddie chose Selected signal. This decision supersedes the initial recommendation
of 01. The chosen direction was vectorized with a dot grid interrupted by one
orange stroke, tall outlined serif capitals, and an italic lower-case "to".

Iris reviewed the actual vector proof and desktop archive preview, grading it
26/28. No load-bearing readability or craft problem was found. Mono and reversed
versions retain their meaning; the dedicated 16px symbol preserves the grid and
dividing line. Typography and craft have optional optical refinement remaining.
Mobile page behavior remains unverified, so this is visual clearance for the
assets and displayed desktop layout rather than a complete mobile QA receipt.
