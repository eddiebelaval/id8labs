What this is.

About

The premise.

Anthropic ships at a pace that's blinding. In three weeks before Issue 01, Anthropic pushed 56 releases across models, Claude Code, SDKs, and research. X and Reddit cover them in fragments. Nobody ties the week together.

Shipped. does. Every Friday at 9 AM ET, one issue, one read. Lead story, feature, investigation, release log. The whole week in order.

The doctrine.

Auto-ops, human-voice. The pipeline scrapes, assembles, verifies, and stages every issue automatically. Nine slash commands drive it, from Monday signal to Friday ship.

The voice is not automated. Every word of every front-of-book section is edited by a person against a voice gate that bans press-release language and enforces a kill list of forbidden phrases.

The gate.

The pipeline stages the issue. A human pushes it live. That sequence does not change. The publish button is not automated. This isn't a philosophical position — it's the structural guarantee that an issue never ships without someone reading it first.
