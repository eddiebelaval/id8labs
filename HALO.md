## Heartbeat

id8Labs runs on its own HALO. The Genome lives in the production path (`genome/`) so every asset is on brand by construction, not corrected after the fact. The studio eats its own dog food: the same envelope we install for a client governs id8Labs itself.

## The rings

- **Genome** (`genome/`): the DNA core. Brand-scoped, outlives any single engagement.
  - `DESIGN.md` how it looks, `VOICE.md` how it sounds, `ETHOS.md` who we are.
  - `genome.json` the machine mirror, `AGENT.md` the enforcement contract, `README.md` the index.
  - The applied DESIGN gene (the React kit and composition rules): `docs/EDITORIAL_SYSTEM.md`.
- **Triad / Exec**: per engagement. id8Labs runs its own build through this repo (the site, the products, Shipped.). Client engagements live under their own roots.

## What the Genome encases

- **Look.** The Shipped editorial design language: warm paper, Fraunces / Archivo / JetBrains Mono, orange disciplined to one or two moments, flat, sharp, light only.
- **Sound.** The builder-to-operator voice; the public-safe lexicon and the internal terms of art kept separate; the hard rules (no em or en dashes, no emojis, never fabricate).
- **Who.** AI system architecture and forward deployment; the stack (primitive chains, AI harnesses, intelligence layers, agents with depth and breadth that create presence, not a chatbot); the custodianship model; the founder (Eddie Belaval, AI System Architect); who we serve; the values; the would-never list; the public disclosure discipline.

## Convention note

A client HALO is one directory at `clients/<brand>/` (the locked convention). The studio's own HALO sits at the brand repo root, with the Genome in `genome/` directly in the production path, because the site is the brand's primary expression and the build agents read from here.

## Operating protocol

- Any agent producing for id8Labs starts at `genome/AGENT.md`.
- Re-attune the Genome only when the brand itself changes. It is the stable core.
- Saving the HALO is committing its files. The human ship gate stays.
