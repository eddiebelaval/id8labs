## Register

A builder who ships, talking to operators who ship. Professional but not corporate, technically precise without jargon, confident without arrogance, helpful without selling. Plain declaratives, earned claims, dry wit. The voice of someone showing you the work, not pitching you on it. Auto-ops, human-voice.

The default frame is the client-facing twin: lead with the plain outcome, name the terms of art one layer down for the reader who wants the architecture. Never open with internal doctrine.

## Rules

- Lead with the conclusion. Say the thing, then support it.
- Active voice. Concrete nouns over abstractions. Cut adjectives that do not carry weight.
- **No em dashes or en dashes. Anywhere. Internal or published.** Use a comma, a period, parentheses, or a restructure. (Ruling 2026-06-08; supersedes the earlier "keep them.")
- **No emojis** in UI, copy, code, or any surface. Use an icon from the kit or an inline SVG.
- **Never fabricate.** No invented testimonials, customer or real-person names, origins, or quotes. Take data from canonical sources. When a name or fact is uncertain, use the safe form or ask.
- Person: "we" for id8Labs, "you" for the reader. Avoid faceless third person.
- Numbers and dates in mono context are facts. Take them from canonical sources, never invent.
- No exclamation marks in body copy. Let the claim stand.

## Do / Don't

| CTA | "Read the latest issue", "Start a conversation" | "Unlock your potential today!" |
| Proof | "4 issues published, 56+ releases tracked" | "Trusted by industry leaders worldwide" |

## Anti-voice

Marketing hype, hedging, and corporate throat-clearing. Anything that sounds auto-generated, anything that oversells, anything a press release would say. If a sentence could appear on any SaaS landing page, it is not us.
