# VOICE.md — Verbal Gene
## id8Labs

> The verbal DNA every word conforms to. Read by the Build and Taste loops so copy is on-brand by construction.

---

## Register

A builder who ships, talking to operators who ship. Professional but not corporate; technically precise without jargon; confident without arrogance; helpful without selling. Plain declaratives, earned claims, dry wit. The voice of someone showing you the work, not pitching you on it — "auto-ops, human-voice."

## Lexicon

- **Say:** forward deployment · primitive chain architecture · human gate · auto-ops, human-voice · ships / shipped · architecture, not tools · operators · the work · proof · drudgery before the work.
- **Never say:** revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, next-generation, world-class, seamless, synergy, unlock, supercharge, 10x, delight, paradigm shift, "we're excited to announce," "in today's fast-paced world," and other press-release filler.
- **Names and terms of art (exact spelling/caps):** id8Labs (lowercase `id8` + `Labs`, one word) · Shipped. (with the orange period) · Composer · DeepStack · Pipeline · LLC Ops · MILO · Lexicon · Foundry · Rune · Parallax · Claude Code · MCP · HALO · Triad · Eddie Belaval · Miami, FL.

## Rules

- Lead with the conclusion. Say the thing, then support it.
- Active voice. Concrete nouns over abstractions. Cut adjectives that don't carry weight.
- Em dashes are on-brand — used for the aside and the pivot. Keep them.
- Person: "we" for id8Labs, "you" for the reader. Avoid faceless third person.
- Numbers and dates in mono context are facts — take them from canonical sources, never invent.
- No exclamation marks in body copy. Let the claim stand.

## Do / Don't

| Situation | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | "The week in Anthropic, *tied together*." | "Revolutionizing how you track AI news!" |
| Product line | "Architecture, not tools." | "The ultimate all-in-one AI platform." |
| CTA | "Read the latest issue" · "Start a conversation" | "Unlock your potential today!" |
| Proof | "4 issues published · 56+ releases tracked" | "Trusted by industry leaders worldwide" |

## Anti-voice

Marketing hype, hedging, and corporate throat-clearing. Anything that sounds auto-generated, anything that oversells, anything a press release would say. If a sentence could appear on any SaaS landing page, it's not us.
